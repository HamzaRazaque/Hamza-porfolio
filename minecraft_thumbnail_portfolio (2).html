Open for Commissions

I make custom Minecraft thumbnails for YouTube creators.

Hi, I'm Hamza. I design custom 3D scene renders made specifically for Minecraft videos.

I focus entirely on the Minecraft space. Instead of basic screenshots or stiff character poses, I build full 3D scenes with custom player rigs, natural lighting, shaders, rain, fog, and clean composition so your thumbnails look good on any screen.

What I Do

1. Custom Character Posing

I pose your player skins tailored to your video concept—whether it's an action battle, a dramatic showdown, or a peaceful survival scene.

2. Lighting & Atmosphere

I add realistic light shafts, dark stormy weather, rain drops, shadows, and glow effects to make characters stand out clearly on YouTube feeds.

3. Clear Framing

I keep the layout simple and uncluttered so viewers instantly understand what your video is about, even on small mobile screens.

4. Fast Turnaround

I deliver files on time so you can stay consistent with your upload schedule.

How It Works

Step 1

Send Your Idea

Message me on Discord or Email with your video title, skin file (.png), and any specific scene idea you have.

Step 2

3D Scene Setup

I create the 3D world, set up character rigs, add custom weapons, configure lighting, and render the scene.

Step 3

Final Delivery

I show you a preview draft. Once you like it, I send the high-resolution file ready to upload to YouTube.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to give you before starting?

Just your Minecraft player skin file (.png), your video title or main idea, and any specific poses or items you want in the thumbnail.

How fast will I get my thumbnail?

Turnaround time depends on the complexity, but most thumbnails are finished within 24 to 48 hours.

Can you handle custom skins, capes, or items?

Yes! I can rig custom skins, 3D weapons like tridents or hammers, shields, armor, and custom capes.
