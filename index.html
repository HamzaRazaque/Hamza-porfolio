My Process & What Makes Me Different

A lot of thumbnail artists rely on pre-made 3D packs or stock poses. I don't. Every scene I build starts from a blank Blender file. I study your video concept, think about what emotion or story the thumbnail needs to communicate, and then build the entire scene — character rig, props, background, lighting — all from the ground up.

Lighting is where I spend the most time. Whether it's the deep purple glow of a storm-lit castle, the harsh shadows of a player hiding behind a tree, or the cosmic swirl of the void beneath an End island — the right lighting is what makes a thumbnail feel real and cinematic rather than flat and dull.

After the 3D render is done, I take everything into Photoshop for colour grading, contrast tuning, and final text layout. The goal is always the same: when someone sees your thumbnail on their YouTube home page, they should instantly want to click it.
